Dynamic Allocation Backtest (V1.7.0)

QQQ’s MACD / RSI / Supertrend + a 250-day trend gate determine the allocation tier. Risk controls include a 20% volatility target, a deep-bear cash exit and the V1.7.0 overextension latch: a close more than 15% above SMA250 caps the volatility-scaled raw target at 1x until extension returns to 10% or less. Overheat cuts skip the three-day confirmation; leverage restoration and other signals retain it. The 10%–15% buffer preserves the previous state, and the existing deadband remains. Execution stays signals at the close of day T, rebalancing at the T+1 open: existing holdings absorb overnight moves, and new holdings earn intraday returns. Performance below includes trading costs. Net exposure varies from 0 (cash) → 1.5x (QQQ+QLD), with defensive cash earning short-term Treasury interest. No rebalancing while the target allocation is unchanged, with a 0.15x deadband.

07Allocation rules

Decision chain: ① a 250-day moving average with hysteresis sets the macro regime → ② in bullish regimes, Supertrend/MACD/RSI scores (0–4) select the base tier → ③ bearish regimes select E/R/D for deep bear, dual reversal or ordinary pullback → ④ RSI≤25 in a bearish macro regime latches a minimum 0.15x floor → ⑤ the 20% volatility target continuously scales the base tier into a raw target → ⑥ activate the 1x cap above SMA250 +15%, release at +10% or less, and preserve state between them → ⑦ skip three-day confirmation only for overheat cuts; confirm restoration and other signals, apply the existing deadband, then execute at the next open. Defensive cash earns short-term Treasury interest. Continuously scales the base tier. Volatility spikes cut leverage; scaling never increases it. Final exposure is continuous and can fall within any tier’s visual band.

  • Macro gate: Exit the bullish regime below the 250-day average; re-enter only 1% above it. Asymmetric hysteresis filters false breakouts near the average.
  • Reversal recovery (V1.5): In a bearish regime, check deep-bear conditions (E/F) first. Otherwise, if Supertrend is bullish and the MACD histogram is >0, select R: base 0.75x × volatility scaling. This restores some exposure earlier than D, without restoring leverage.
  • Oversold floor (V1.6): RSI(14)≤25 in a bearish macro regime activates a latch, keeping final net exposure at least 0.15x until the macro regime turns bullish. The floor buys only unleveraged QQQ; R/D targets above 0.15x remain unchanged.
  • Momentum score (bullish regime): Bullish Supertrend +1, MACD histogram >0 +1, MACD line >0 +1, RSI>50 +1; 0–2 points → all QQQ, 3–4 → QQQ+QLD (1.5x cap, MAX_EXPOSURE=1.5).
  • Volatility targeting (core): Raw target = tier exposure ×min(1.0, 20%÷20-day annualized volatility), with a default target range of 0–1.5x (only scaled down). Then apply the SMA250 overextension cap before rounding, confirmation and deadband.
  • Deep-bear handling: E/F requires price 4% below the 250-day average, MACD <0 and bearish Supertrend. Without the oversold latch, hold 100% cash; with it, retain a 0.15x floor. Shallow breaches remain D/R.
  • Confirmation and execution: Round exposure to 0.05 increments and require 3 consecutive days of confirmation, then apply a 0.15x deadband (no change if target differs from current holdings by <0.15x).
